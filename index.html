Activities & Events in Kuwait: A Year-Round Experience

Discover Kuwait All Year Long

Kuwait is a destination filled with culture, modern attractions, and unforgettable experiences in every season. Whether you visit during the warm summer months or the cool, vibrant winter season, Kuwait offers a rich mix of activities, events, and entertainment for every type of traveler. Here is your complete guide to enjoying Kuwait throughout the year.

☀️ Summer Activities in Kuwait

1. Indoor Cultural Explorations

Beat the heat by stepping into Kuwait’s world-class museums and cultural centers. The Sheikh Abdullah Al-Salem Cultural Centre, one of the largest museum complexes in the world, takes visitors on a journey through space, science, natural history, and Arabic Islamic heritage. The Kuwait National Museum and Al Shaheed Park Museums offer immersive insights into the nation’s story.
Why it’s great for summer: Cool, spacious, and interactive environments ideal for families, students, and culture lovers.

2. Waterfront Adventures

As the sun dips below the Gulf, Kuwait’s coastline becomes the perfect place to unwind. Visitors can enjoy jet skiing, boating, sunset cruises, or a relaxing walk along the Arabian Gulf Street Corniche.
Why it’s great for summer: Outdoor fun with cooler temperatures after sunset.

3. Shopping, Dining & Entertainment

Kuwait is home to one of the Middle East’s most impressive shopping experiences — The Avenues Mall. This massive destination is not just a mall; it’s a world of its own. It features a variety of uniquely designed districts, each with its own architectural theme: the high-end elegance of Prestige, the European-inspired Grand Avenue, the cultural vibe of The Souk, and the modern style of The Forum.
Perfect for summer: Fully air-conditioned with endless entertainment options.

4. Summer Resort & Pool Experiences

Many hotels and clubs offer refreshing poolside escapes, private cabanas, and family water activities. Resorts turn into lively social spots with music nights, themed buffets, and spa packages.
Why it’s great for summer: Refreshing, relaxing experiences designed for warm days.

❄️ Winter Activities & Events in Kuwait

1. Hala February Festival

Hala February is one of Kuwait’s most iconic and meaningful annual celebrations. Held every February, it marks a month of national pride, joy, and cultural unity. The festival is deeply meaningful because it coincides with two of Kuwait’s most important national holidays:

  • Kuwait National Day – February 25: Celebrates the founding of the modern Kuwaiti state.
  • Kuwait Liberation Day – February 26: Commemorates the liberation of Kuwait in 1991.

During Hala February, the country bursts into life

  • Colorful parades and decorations
  • Huge fireworks shows
  • Outdoor concerts
  • Mall festivals and shopping promotions
  • Cultural performances & food events

2. Outdoor Festivals & Cultural Seasons

Winter is the heart of Kuwait’s event calendar. Visitors can enjoy large-scale festivals like Winter Wonderland Kuwait, Gardenia Season, and cultural events hosted at Al Shaheed Park.

3. Island Trips & Coastal Activities

With mild weather and calm seas, winter is the ideal time to explore Kuwait’s natural side. Travelers can take boat trips to Kubbar Island for snorkeling and swimming or explore Green Island’s restaurants and entertainment areas.

4. Desert Adventures

Winter opens the door to Kuwait’s desert experiences. Visitors can enjoy camel riding, desert camp nights, stargazing, sandboarding, and 4x4 dune adventures.

5. Sports, Nature & Parks

The cooler season makes Kuwait’s parks and reserves come alive. Jahra Pools Nature Reserve offers birdwatching and natural scenery, while Al Shaheed Park provides walking trails, gardens, and outdoor cafés.
